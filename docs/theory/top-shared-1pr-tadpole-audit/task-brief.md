1. **Question.** Compute the complete common one-loop tadpole vector within the approximations of NST (A54)–(A55) at the 350 GeV benchmark, and check its exact paired contribution to (A30). Distinguish the local loop-minimum shift and the pole-defined VEV conversion. Exclude other one-loop self-energies, a full electroweak match, thermal matching and thermal observables.

4. **Conventions.** Zero-temperature 4D real singlet with zero tree singlet VEV, positive inverse-propagator NST convention, MS-bar, xi=1 with dimension-dependent numerator expanded before subtraction. 7. **Independent derivation.** Implement printed one-point diagrams and cubic vertices in the NST signed basis. Separately differentiate the one-loop field-dependent potential using the CP-even Hessian eigenvalues, gauge, Goldstone/ghost and top masses and solve a two-field linear response. Then project the same common tadpole through A28/A29/A30 and the VEV relation.
8. **Acceptance.** Compare each signed h1/h2 tadpole and topology with independent potential derivatives; scaled error <1e-36 at 65 digits with two finite steps. Confirm opposite-signed local versus matching VEV shifts; the exact A30 cancellation (absolute residual <1e-55), and A28/A29 nulls. Byte replay, real locked standards validation and RCRPS v1.0.1 manifest pass. CI not requested.
