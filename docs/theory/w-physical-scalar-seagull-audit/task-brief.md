# Research Task Brief: physical-scalar W seagulls

Task 20261123-w-physical-scalar-seagull, pre-execution, 23 November UTC.

1. Isolate the two momentum-independent physical-Higgs 1PI seagulls in NST (A58) for the fixed published 350 GeV point. Test whether these plus the already verified mixed bubbles close the W 1PI subset containing an internal physical Higgs.
2. Sources: NST Phys. Rev. D 103, 115035, arXiv:2103.07467v3 §II, (A20),(A26)–(A30),(A36)–(A37),(A58) and APS erratum; Ramsey-Musolf, Tenkanen and Tran JHEP09(2026)260 §4.1 via prior pinned inputs.
3. Baseline clean detached 9116236f39517641eec98fa6d718ca0d4b4f207c; main 5d354669a2c46da1d53b4560476f3981493ed7be, old Draft #1 b1ba61e6a27224696822db20367da2e364e9e33b. Preserve all refs.
4. Zero-temperature xSM, tree minimum with zero singlet VEV, Feynman gauge and MS-bar in NST inverse-propagator sign. Pole inputs and scales fixed by prior audit; g from Gmu. No expansion in mixing.
5. Only internal h1/h2 contractions of quartic W+W−hihj, not charged/neutral Goldstones, common 1PR tadpoles, Z or top parts. This is a gauge-fixed bookkeeping subset.
6. Existing mixed-bubble and shared-1PR calculations are immutable. If combined, use signed bubble totals from exactly pinned native output only, at matched exact scales.
7. Derive coefficient independently by contracting the Hessian of the gauge-boson mass with the rotated scalar propagators. Compare A36 with a Euclidean proper-time regulator converted explicitly to MS-bar; prove and check finite-cutoff error, and test omission of the required finite conversion.
8. Acceptance: signed agreement bounded at each cutoff for both masses at all three matched scales; high-cutoff difference below 1e−48 GeV²; analytic scale slope, zero-mix and degeneracy checks, A28 exactly zero, A30 W-only signed. Replay native result byte-for-byte and validate standards/RCRPS. Zero inclusion of 1PR.
9. Archive derivation, source, immutable input hashes, tests, raw signed output, commands/logs, manifest and task provenance. Local stage only.
10. Other bosonic W, all new Z components, full EW pole matching and any thermal EFT, transition, bounce, GW predictions remain outside this increment.
